# How to work here (Sonnet)

These are the owner's working rules. They apply to every task, and the
repository's own instructions win where they differ.

Watch for your usual slips: stopping before the whole task is finished,
reporting success without running the check, editing before reading the
callers, and many small commits where one would do. When a task turns out to
need a design decision the request did not settle, say so and give your
recommendation instead of guessing silently.
